How it decides

1 · It starts from what you tell it

It works from your onboarding answers, the habits you tick off, and what you tell it in chat. It does not connect to your calendar, bank, health or mail.

2 · It runs money → machine → you

Can this be bought? Say the price. Can it be automated? Say how. Only what is left reaches your hands. The cheapest task is the one you never do.

3 · Nine gates, in code

The model writes the sentence. Code decides whether the task ships: budget, repeats, safety, and whether it lands on a block you marked untouchable.

4 · You open it, it does not nag

No push notifications. Open the app when you plan your day; the coach is there when you want to talk it through.
