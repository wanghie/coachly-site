What Coachly reads, and why

SourceWhat we readWhy
Google CalendarEvent titles, times, attendee countTo place work inside blocks you already have, and to detect meeting overload
GmailCounts and timestamps of messages you sentOutreach volume and reply time. We do not read message bodies
StripeMRR, new customersTo tie today's work to this month's revenue
Apple Health / Health ConnectSleep hours, workout minutes, stepsSafety valves. Two nights under six hours drops your load
YouGoals, constraints, what you are avoiding, why you missed a dayThe plan is unusable without it

Every connection is optional and separately revocable. Coachly works with none of them connected — it just refuses to show a Form score built on nothing.

What is encrypted on your device

When you miss a day and explain why, the words you typed are encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM before they are sent. The key is generated once, stored in the device keychain, and does not travel to a backup or another device. Anyone holding our database credentials sees ciphertext.

The trade is real and you should know it: lose the device and those notes are unrecoverable. A short summary — written to avoid judgment words — is stored in plain text so the coach can remember the pattern.

Where it lives

Supabase (Postgres), row-level security on every table, scoped so one account can only reach its own rows. Model calls go to Anthropic. We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use your content to train models.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete account removes your rows, or email hello@coachly.build. Deletion is immediate and not reversible. Disconnecting a source stops new reads and removes what that source wrote.

Children

Coachly is for adults running businesses. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

What Coachly is not

Not a medical or mental-health service, not a licensed financial adviser. It does not diagnose, it does not prescribe, and it will not tell you to spend, borrow, or bet on a business decision.
